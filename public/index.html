Measurement notes

Outlines. Case width and height use published front-view dimensions. The inner line is the active display on nine watches. Apple Watch Ultra 4 uses its published front-glass boundary.

Illustrations. Button type, count and placement follow manufacturer references. Button protrusion, lug shape, band length and exact case contours are approximate. Apple and Samsung band widths are illustrative.

Thickness. Side profiles use published depth and case height. Balance 3 includes its sensor; its case alone is 12.5 mm. T-Rex 3 and PACE 4 published depths exclude the sensor. Bezel, case, back and strap contours are simplified from manufacturer imagery, not engineering drawings; makers may measure depth differently.
